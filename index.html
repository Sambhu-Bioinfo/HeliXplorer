DNA Molecular Architecture Guide

1. Antiparallel Strands (5' to 3')

The DNA double helix consists of two directional strands running in opposite directions. One strand runs 5' to 3', while its complement runs 3' to 5'.

2. Watson-Crick Base Pairing Rules

Nucleotides pair specifically across strands via hydrogen bonds:

  • Adenine (A) pairs with Thymine (T) via 2 Hydrogen Bonds.
  • Guanine (G) pairs with Cytosine (C) via 3 Hydrogen Bonds.

3. Helical Geometry (B-DNA Model)

Standard B-DNA completes a full 360° helical turn every 10.5 base pairs, with a helical pitch of ~3.4 nm and major/minor grooves regulating protein interaction.
